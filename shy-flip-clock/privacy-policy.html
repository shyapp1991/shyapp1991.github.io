Privacy Policy

Shy Flip Clock (the "App") is published by withshy. Effective date: October 5, 2026.

Summary

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking, and it does not request internet access.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or usage analytics.

Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the App stores your preferences (such as clock style, display options, brightness level, and Pomodoro duration) locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the App. If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include these settings in your own backup under your Google account, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

The App does not request any special permissions. It adjusts the brightness of its own window only while it is open and keeps the screen on while the clock is displayed.

Third-party services

The App contains no advertising, analytics, or other third-party service SDKs. It includes open source libraries and fonts that run entirely on your device; their licenses are listed in the App under Settings > About > Open source licenses.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
